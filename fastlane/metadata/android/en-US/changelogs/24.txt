Radarr monitors collections: adding a movie can monitor its whole collection,
and the movie screen has a button for it.

qBittorrent marks torrents as private or public, in the list on 5.0 and later
and on the torrent screen from 4.5.1. A profile can be renamed from its card.

Ombi has a new look, with Requests and Discover tabs, counts on the filters and
richer cards. Navidrome is out of beta, with playlists as a grid of covers and
the artist across an album's banner. Lidarr and Unraid are out of beta too.

Fixed: Ombi request times were off by the timezone, Navidrome artist headers
fit the server's longer group names, and its cover grids fit at large text sizes.
