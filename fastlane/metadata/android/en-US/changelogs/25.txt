A new service: AdGuard Home, in beta. Turn protection on or off or pause it
for a time, see the DNS statistics and the top clients and domains, search the
query log and block or unblock from it, and give a client its own settings. A
dashboard widget shows the state. Filter lists and settings are still to come.

Sonarr and Radarr offer the custom filters saved on your server in the sort
and filter sheet.
