Unmatched Depth

Atrium isn't just a dashboard. It's a fully-featured controller for every service.

The *arr Stack
  • Sonarr & Radarr: Full settings editor, calendar, advanced sorting, and filtering.
  • Lidarr: Artists, albums and tracks, wanted, activity, settings, and an in-app log reader.
  • Prowlarr: Add indexers, manual search & grab, history, and system status.
  • Bazarr: Manage wanted subtitles, manual search, and provider details.
Media Servers
  • Plex: Explore libraries, genres, now-playing controller, and music.
  • Jellyfin & Emby: Remote control capabilities, live sessions, libraries, and music.
  • Tautulli: 30-day stats, detailed history, and active stream termination.
  • Tracearr: Live sessions across Plex, Jellyfin and Emby, watch and library stats, history, and a map of where accounts are streaming from.
Downloads & Utilities
  • qBittorrent: Real-time torrent list, add/manage, per-file priority, and tracker details.
  • Deluge, Transmission & rTorrent: Live lists, start/stop and priorities, speed limits, and per-torrent files, peers and trackers.
  • SABnzbd & NZBGet: Queue control with reorder, priority and categories, plus history with retry.
  • Seerr: Discover new media, search, and manage requests (approve/decline/retry).
  • Glances: Live CPU, memory, network, and disk monitoring gauges.
  • Speedtest Tracker: Latest result, history chart, and remote test runs.
  • Beszel: Systems list, live metrics, and a per-system detail screen.
  • dashdot: Live CPU, memory, disk, and GPU usage with a system-info tab.
  • Unraid: Array and disk health, per-core system load, Docker containers, and virtual machine control.
